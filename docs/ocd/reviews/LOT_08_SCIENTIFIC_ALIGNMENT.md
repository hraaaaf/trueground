# LOT 08 — Scientific Alignment Review

Project: TrueGround OCD
Date: 2026-09-19
Scope: Values-oriented redirection + human-support route
Status: DESIGN-DIRECTION REVIEWED; NO TREATMENT CLAIM AUTHORIZED

## Question

Can TrueGround offer a short user-led `Return to what matters` flow and a route to real people without presenting those features as treatment or reassurance?

## Findings

1. ERP/CBT remains the established core treatment direction for OCD. LOT08 must not present a values flow as a replacement for ERP.
2. Recent ACT literature describes valued action and reduced experiential avoidance as potentially useful complementary processes in OCD.
3. A randomized trial comparing ERP with ACT+ERP found both conditions improved, with no superiority of ACT+ERP. This argues against any stronger efficacy claim for the LOT08 values flow.
4. Systematic reviews report promising ACT results, while also noting evidence-quality and generalizability limitations.
5. Experiential avoidance is associated with OCD and related symptoms, but association does not prove that this exact TrueGround interaction treats OCD.
6. NICE supports appropriate involvement of families/carers and professional care, while explicitly warning that carers can become involved in avoidance or reassurance seeking. A support flow should therefore favor presence/practical support over repeated certainty-giving.

## Product transfer

### Supported as design direction

- user chooses the valued area;
- app redirects toward a small real-life action;
- uncertainty does not need to be eliminated before action;
- support from real people remains visible;
- trusted-person copy avoids recruiting others into repetitive reassurance.

### Not supported as a claim

LOT08 must not claim that:

- the values flow treats OCD;
- the flow is validated ACT;
- it is equivalent or superior to ERP;
- it reduces symptoms or anxiety;
- it prevents compulsions or relapse;
- a trusted-person interaction is therapeutic by itself.

## Evidence

1. Lee EB. Acceptance and Commitment Therapy for Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder. Psychiatr Clin North Am. 2025;48(3):457-466. PMID: 40738526. DOI: 10.1016/j.psc.2025.02.004.
2. Twohig MP et al. Adding acceptance and commitment therapy to exposure and response prevention for obsessive-compulsive disorder: a randomized controlled trial. Behav Res Ther. 2018. PMID: 29966992.
3. A Systematic Review of the Use of Acceptance and Commitment Therapy to Treat Adult Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder. 2023. PMID: 37863582.
4. Angelakis I, Pseftogianni F. Association between obsessive-compulsive and related disorders and experiential avoidance: systematic review and meta-analysis. J Psychiatr Res. 2021;138:228-239. PMID: 33866051.
5. NICE CG31. Obsessive-compulsive disorder and body dysmorphic disorder: treatment. Recommendations 1.1.4 and 1.5.2, including family/carer involvement and reduction of reassurance/avoidance accommodation.

## Verdict

The external evidence is sufficient to support the conservative LOT08 design direction as a non-treatment, user-led product flow.

It is not sufficient to validate TrueGround itself as an ACT intervention or to authorize efficacy claims. Exact copy and digital ritualization risk remain TrueGround-specific and require targeted product/safety review.
